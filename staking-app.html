How custody actually works

Your vault is a smart account on Ethereum that you own. When you join, the app creates it for you — no MetaMask, no browser extensions. Day to day you approve actions with a passkey (fingerprint or face unlock). Behind it sits a 12-word recovery phrase that only you hold — the on-chain recovery key. Fenix servers only ever see your vault's public address.

Staking runs through a Fenix automation wallet that holds an on-chain permission (Zodiac Roles) on your vault, and Fenix holds that wallet's key. The permission limits which contracts it can reach and refuses a direct ETH send to any other address. It does not limit every action on those contracts, so a stolen automation key could move funds out of your vault.

  • Withdrawals are always open. Cancel your subscription and your ETH stays yours, fully withdrawable.
  • No copy-to-clipboard for your recovery phrase, and screenshots are blocked while it's on screen — small details, because the phrase is the whole ballgame.

Frequently asked questions

Who holds my ETH in Fenix Yield?

Your ETH sits in a smart vault on Ethereum that you own, not in a company account. Staking runs through a Fenix automation wallet that holds a permission on your vault, and Fenix holds that wallet's key.

Does Fenix Yield hold my keys?

No. You unlock the app with a passkey, and your 12-word recovery phrase — which only you hold — is the on-chain recovery key for your vault. Fenix servers only ever see the vault's public address. Fenix does hold the key to its own automation wallet, which is separate from yours.

What can Fenix's automation actually do with my ETH?

It stakes idle ETH through an on-chain permission (Zodiac Roles) on your vault. The permission limits which contracts it can reach and refuses a direct ETH send to any other address, but it does not limit every action on those contracts. So a stolen automation key could move funds out of your vault. Withdrawals are always open to you.

Which staking protocols are supported?

Five: Lido, Rocket Pool, Aave, Curve, and Convex — with each pool's live rate shown in the app.
