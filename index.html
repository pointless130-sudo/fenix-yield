Custody, in plain terms

Your vault is yours. Here's exactly what Fenix can do.

Your passkey
Opens your vault day to day. In the app, withdrawals and sends to any other wallet need your approval with it.
Your 12-word phrase
The on-chain recovery key for your vault. Only you hold it. Fenix's servers never see the phrase, only its address.
Fenix's automation
Stakes idle ETH into the pools you've enabled, using a wallet whose key Fenix holds and an on-chain permission (Zodiac Roles). That permission doesn't limit every action, so a stolen key could move funds out of your vault.
Fenix AI
Answers questions about your vault and the pools. It can suggest an action, but only you can approve it.
If your membership ends
Your funds keep earning and withdrawals stay open.
