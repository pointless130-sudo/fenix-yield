Security guide

What protects your ETH, and what you need to protect.

Your ETH sits in a vault you own on Ethereum, not in a Fenix account. That means your phone and your recovery phrase matter, and so does the automation permission explained below.

Who can do what

Your passkey
Opens your vault day to day. In the app, withdrawals and sends to any other wallet need your approval with it, using your fingerprint, face or screen lock.
Your 12-word phrase
The on-chain recovery key for your vault. Only you hold it. Fenix's servers never see the phrase, only its address.
Fenix's automation
Stakes idle ETH into the pools you've enabled, using a wallet whose key Fenix holds and an on-chain permission. That permission doesn't limit every action, so a stolen key could move funds out of your vault.
Fenix AI
Answers questions about your vault and the pools. It can suggest an action, but only you can approve it.
Fenix Labs
Writes the software and runs the automation wallet. We don't hold your funds, your passkey or your phrase.

How the automation permission works

Your vault is a Safe smart account on Ethereum. It comes with a Zodiac Roles module, an audited, open-source permissions contract from Gnosis Guild. Fenix's automation wallet holds one role in that module.

  • Limited to certain contracts. The role can only reach the contracts of the pools your vault has enabled, and a direct ETH send to any other address is rejected on-chain.
  • The module belongs to your vault. After setup, Fenix can't widen its own permissions. Adding Rocket Pool takes your passkey approval.
  • You set how it behaves. Auto-stake can be Daily, Weekly or Off. In Settings you can also set a Switch gap, a Minimum to invest and a Weekly limit.
  • Not limited to certain actions. On those contracts the role can call any function, including token transfers and an Aave deposit credited to someone else. Fenix's software only sends staking calls with it, but the contract doesn't enforce that. So if our servers were compromised, funds could be moved out of your vault.

No software is free of risk, and these limits don't protect you from the risks listed below. Developers can read our Zodiac Roles field guide for the technical detail.

Protect your recovery phrase and your phone

  • Write the 12 words on paper and keep them offline. The app has no copy button for the phrase and blocks screenshots while it's on screen, because copies on a phone or in the cloud can be stolen.
  • Never share them. Don't type them into a website, a chat or another app, including Fenix AI. Nobody from Fenix will ever ask for them.
  • Keep a screen lock on your phone. The app needs one to create a passkey, and it's what stands between a lost phone and your approvals.
  • Install only from the real source. Use Google Play. Outside the US, Fenix Intel members can download it directly.

What Fenix will never do

  • Ask for your 12-word phrase or your passkey.
  • Ask you to send ETH to "verify", "unlock" or "upgrade" your vault. Deposits go only to your own vault address, which the app shows you.
  • Contact you out of the blue with an urgent warning that you'll lose your funds.
  • Offer support from any email address other than support@fenix-yield.com.

Not sure about a message? Don't tap anything in it. Open the app yourself, or email support@fenix-yield.com.

Check it for yourself

  • Look up your vault. In the app, Settings has a View Safe on Etherscan link to your vault's public record.
  • Watch your activity. Every withdrawal or send adds a Vault activity alert to the app's notification center. You can't turn these off.
  • Choose your other alerts. Rate alerts, better opportunities, stETH price warnings and compound confirmations are switches in Settings.

The risks that remain

  • Smart contracts can fail. The pools are third-party contracts that can have bugs or be exploited, and that could lose funds.
  • Fenix's automation key could be stolen. The permission above doesn't limit every action, so a thief with that key could move funds out of your vault.
  • ETH's value moves. Its price goes up and down, and the app doesn't protect you from that.
  • Pool tokens can drift. stETH can trade below ETH, which can affect what you get when you withdraw.
  • Rates change daily. Yields are variable and not guaranteed, and network fees can exceed what a small position earns.
  • Your phrase is on you. Fenix can't recover it for you.
