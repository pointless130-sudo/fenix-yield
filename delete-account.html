Delete Your Account

Fenix Yield  ·  Last updated: July 15, 2026

In short: You can ask us to delete your Fenix Yield account and the personal data we hold at any time by emailing support@fenix-yield.com. Because Fenix Yield is a self-custodial wallet, your funds and keys live only on your device — deleting your account never touches them, and they remain entirely yours.

How to request deletion

To request deletion of your Fenix Yield account and its associated personal data:

  1. Open the app and go to Settings to find your wallet address (tap to copy it). This is how we identify your account — we don't use email or usernames for the mobile app.
  2. Email support@fenix-yield.com from any email address, with your wallet address in the message, and ask us to delete your account. Telegram users can instead include their Telegram username.
  3. We'll confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days, and email you when it's done.

What we delete

On request, we delete the account record and the personal data we hold that is associated with your account, including:

Data Deleted
Account record (wallet address, passkey public key)Yes
Subscription record & entitlement statusYes
Referral data tied to your accountYes
Push notification token & alert preferencesYes
Support / contact info and any email you shared with usYes
Diagnostics / analytics associated with your accountYes

What we cannot or do not delete

  • Your wallet and funds. Fenix Yield is self-custodial — your private keys and recovery phrase live only on your device, never on our servers. We can't delete your wallet, and deleting your account does not affect your funds. To stop using Fenix, simply move your funds out and uninstall the app; your wallet remains yours and recoverable with your 12-word phrase.
  • Public blockchain data. Transactions and balances recorded on the Ethereum blockchain are permanent, public, and outside our control. They cannot be deleted by anyone.
  • Records we're legally required to keep. See retention below.

Data retention

We delete the personal data listed above within 30 days of a verified request. Some records are retained longer only where we are legally required to keep them:

  • Payment and transaction records may be retained for up to 7 years where required for tax, accounting, and legal-compliance purposes. Where possible these are anonymized or disassociated from your identity.
  • Backups. Residual copies may persist in encrypted backups for a short period until those backups rotate out (typically within 30 days), after which they are permanently overwritten.

If you have any questions about deletion or retention, contact us at support@fenix-yield.com — see our Privacy Policy for full details.
