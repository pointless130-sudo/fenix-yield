Questions about pricing

How do I pay?

You subscribe inside the app, and Google Play bills you and manages the subscription.

Who gets the first-year price?

New subscribers. It appears automatically at checkout, so there's no code to enter. It's for Google accounts that haven't had this subscription before. Other accounts pay $38.99/month.

Does it renew automatically?

Yes, every month. You can cancel anytime in your Google Play account, and you keep access until the end of the current month.

What happens to my ETH if my membership ends?

It stays in your vault. Your funds keep earning and withdrawals stay open. A membership unlocks the app's full features; it doesn't hold your ETH.

Are there fees besides the membership?

Every on-chain action pays an Ethereum network fee, which changes with how busy the network is. The app shows the exact fee before you confirm and deducts it from that transaction at cost, with no markup. Your first stake and one withdrawal each calendar month are covered up to a capped amount. If a fee would be more than 5% of the amount you're moving, the app stops and suggests waiting or moving a larger amount.

What will my ETH earn?

We quote a range of 1.5–5% a year across the five pools. Rates move a lot, and any pool can fall below that range. The app's Markets tab shows live rates. Yields are variable and not guaranteed. Not financial advice.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases through Google Play are refunded under Google Play's policy. See our refund policy for details.

I'm outside the US. Can I use it?

Google Play has the app in the US for now. Fenix Intel members anywhere can download it directly and pay in crypto in the app, for a fixed term that doesn't renew automatically.
