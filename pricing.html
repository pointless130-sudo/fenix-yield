Common questions

Pricing FAQ

What's the difference between Autopilot and the Mobile App?

Autopilot ($19) runs on Telegram and uses your own MetaMask + Gnosis Safe — best if you're comfortable with crypto and want the lowest price. Mobile App ($39) creates a secure wallet for you and does everything in one tap — best if you're new to crypto and want zero setup. Both are fully non-custodial.

How do I pay?

Telegram Autopilot: pay by card or crypto (ETH/USDC/USDT) directly in the bot. Mobile App: subscriptions are billed through Google Play (and the Samsung Galaxy Store at launch) and managed in your store account. A direct Android build is also available from the website for those who prefer to install outside the store.

Does my subscription auto-renew?

Subscriptions purchased through Google Play or Samsung Galaxy Store renew automatically and are managed in that store's settings — cancel anytime there. Subscriptions purchased directly (Telegram or the direct Android build) are for a fixed period (1, 3, 6, or 12 months) and do not auto-renew — you simply re-subscribe when you're ready.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Direct plans stay active until the period you paid for ends — nothing recurs. Store subscriptions can be cancelled in your Google Play or Samsung account and stay active until the end of the current cycle.

Do you offer refunds?

Direct purchases include a 7-day money-back guarantee on your first payment if the software doesn't work as described — see our Refund Policy. Purchases made through Google Play or Samsung are refunded under that store's refund policy.

Do coupon codes work on both paid plans?

Yes — coupon codes apply a discount to Autopilot or the Mobile App. Enter your code above to preview the discounted price before you subscribe.

Are there any fees besides the subscription?

Every on-chain action — staking, switching pools, withdrawing — pays a small Ethereum network (gas) fee, usually just a few cents at current gas. Mobile App: we show the exact fee in ETH and USD before you confirm and deduct it from that transaction — Fenix adds no markup. Your first stake after setup and one withdrawal each month are on us. Telegram Autopilot: gas is paid from your own Safe as usual. The subscription price never changes.

What does Fenix Yield actually do?

Fenix Yield is a software automation tool. It monitors DeFi protocol APIs, finds the highest available yield across Lido, Curve, Aave and Convex, and stakes your ETH there — either by proposing a transaction you approve, or automatically within strict on-chain limits you control. It never holds, manages, or has access to your funds. Yield is variable (typically 1.5–5% APY) and not guaranteed.
