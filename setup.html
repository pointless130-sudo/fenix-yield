How it works

Using Fenix Yield, start to finish.

No MetaMask and no browser extensions. The app creates your vault for you, and you approve what matters with your phone's passkey.

Set it up

  1. Get your phone ready

    You need Android 10 or newer, a screen lock and a Google account on the phone. Without a screen lock, your phone can't create a passkey. The 2-minute phone guide has the exact menus.

  2. Install the app

    Get Fenix Yield on Google Play, available in the US. Outside the US, Fenix Intel members can download it directly.

  3. Create your wallet

    Open the app and follow the steps to create your vault. If someone gave you a referral code, enter it here.

    The app shows a 12-word recovery phrase. Write it on paper and keep it somewhere safe. You'll confirm two of the words, then approve with your fingerprint, face or screen lock.

  4. Start your membership

    Subscribe in the app through Google Play. It renews monthly, and you can cancel anytime in Google Play. See pricing.

  5. Deposit at least 0.01 ETH

    The app shows your vault address. Send ETH to it on the Ethereum network from any wallet or exchange. Your vault activates automatically after that first deposit, which can take a few minutes.

    Don't have ETH yet? Venmo and Robinhood both sell ETH and send it on Ethereum by default. In Venmo, turn on crypto transfers first. On an exchange, choose the Ethereum network when you withdraw, never Base, Arbitrum, Optimism or Polygon. Buy a little extra to cover their fees. These are third-party services we're not affiliated with.

  6. Stake your ETH

    Open the stake sheet and pick a pool, or choose Auto and Fenix uses the pool with the highest rate when you stake, leaving out Convex, which earns about what Curve does at higher network fees. Tap Stake Now and confirm.

    To have new deposits staked for you, set Auto-stake to Daily or Weekly. It starts Off, so nothing is staked until you choose. Lido, Aave, Curve and Convex are ready to use, and Rocket Pool unlocks with one passkey approval.

Use it day to day

  • Home shows your balance and where it's staked.
  • Markets compares each pool's current rate.
  • Portfolio shows what you've earned over time, and you can export your rewards as a spreadsheet.
  • Chat is Fenix AI, which answers questions about your vault and the pools. It can suggest an action, but only you can approve it.
  • Settings holds your alerts and your automation rules.

Get your ETH out

  1. Withdraw from the pool

    On Home, under In your vault, tap the pool and choose Withdraw to vault for all or part of it. It comes back to your vault as ETH, approved with your passkey.

  2. Send it to another wallet

    Use Send ETH and approve with your passkey. Only ETH that's available in your vault can be sent, so withdraw it from the pool first.

If your membership ends, your funds keep earning and withdrawals stay open. Ethereum network fees apply, and the app shows the fee before you confirm.
