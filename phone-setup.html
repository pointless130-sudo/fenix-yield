Mobile App

Get your phone ready

The Fenix Yield app protects your money with your fingerprint or face — so your phone needs a couple of things set up first. Takes about 2 minutes.

Why this matters: your Fenix wallet is secured by a passkey — a key that lives on your phone and unlocks with your fingerprint, face, or screen lock. There's no password and no seed phrase to type. But if your phone doesn't have a screen lock or a Google account set up, it can't create a passkey, and the app can't create your wallet. The steps below fix that before you even install.
1

Set a screen lock Required

Your phone must have a screen lock — a PIN, pattern, or password. Best: also add your fingerprint or face unlock, so approving transactions is one touch.

Settings → Security & privacy → Screen lock  ·  then → Fingerprint (or Face unlock)
2

Be signed into a Google account Required

Your passkey is stored safely by Google on your phone, and the app installs through Google Play — both need a Google account signed in on the device.

Settings → Passwords & accounts → Add account → Google
3

Make sure Google can save passkeys Required

On most phones this is already on. If wallet creation ever says "no create options available," this is the switch to check — make sure Google is enabled as a passwords & passkeys service.

Settings → Passwords & accounts → Passkeys (or "Preferred service") → Google ✓
4

Check your Android version Required

The app needs Android 10 or newer (phones from roughly 2019 onward). Older phones can't run it.

Settings → About phone → Android version
5

Update Google Play Recommended

An out-of-date Google Play can cause install or passkey hiccups. Open the Play Store, tap your profile picture → Settings → About → Update Play Store, and install any pending updates under Manage apps & device.

6

Allow notifications when asked Recommended

When the app first opens it will ask to send notifications. Say yes — this is how you get security alerts (any time money leaves your wallet) and yield updates. You can fine-tune which alerts you get later in the app's Settings.

If something goes wrong

"Your phone isn't set up to create passkeys" / "No create options available"

This means steps 1–3 above aren't complete — most often there's no screen lock set, or no Google account on the phone. Complete those steps, then reopen the app and tap Try again. Your recovery phrase is not affected.

Can't find the app on Google Play

If you're a tester: open your invite link in Chrome first and accept it — and make sure the Play Store is signed into the same Google account the invite was sent to (Play Store → profile picture, top right, to check or switch). New releases can also take up to ~30 minutes to appear; clearing the Play Store cache (Settings → Apps → Google Play Store → Clear cache) speeds it up.

Fingerprint prompt never appears

Add a fingerprint under Settings → Security & privacy → Fingerprint. If your phone has no fingerprint reader, a PIN or pattern works too — the app will ask for that instead.

Still stuck?

Ask the Setup Assistant — it can walk you through these steps one at a time — or email support@fenix-yield.com.

✓ Phone ready?

Install Fenix Yield from Google Play, open it, and tap Create wallet — you'll write down a 12-word backup phrase, confirm it, and approve with your fingerprint. Your wallet is yours alone from that moment.
